Rock Solid Support

A Benefit Cruise for a Local North Georgia Road Builder

Charity Fundraiser Cruise

Saturday, November 28, 2026

Two days after Thanksgiving and the day after Black Friday!

"He helps build the roads we travel. Now it's our turn to pave the way for him."

Why We're Banding Together

North Georgia Street heaters is hosting this special charity cruise to support a local North Georgia road builder who is facing serious personal challenges.

He has spent his time helping build the roads we travel every day. Now it's our turn to come together and show him that his community stands behind him during this challenging time, especially with it being during the holidays!

Whether you participate in the cruise, join us for the car meet afterward, or make a donation, every contribution is appreciated!

Meeting Information

⏱️Meeting Time: 9:30 AM

🚗Cruise Departure: 10:00 AM

🏁Estimated Return: 11:30 AM

Location:
Yahoola Creek Park
1166 Captain McDonald Road
Dahlonega, GA 30533

Please arrive at 9:30 AM so we can coordinate the cruise, organize everyone's positions, and go over a few things before departure.

Entry & Donations

Entry Fee: $25 per vehicle (cash only)

Additional donations are greatly appreciated!

Cruise Route

We'll leave Yahoola Creek Park, take the Dahlonega Bypass towards Dawsonville, continue through downtown Dawsonville, and return to Dahlonega using GA-53, GA-400, and GA-60.

Car Meet After the Cruise!

Join us back at Yahoola Creek Park for a car meet immediately following the cruise!

All cars, trucks, motorcycles, scooters, vans, SUVs, and spectators are welcome!
