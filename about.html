About North Georgia Street Heaters

🚗 How It All Started

North Georgia Street Heaters started with a simple thought: Why should we have to drive all over Georgia just to find a car meet?

After spending plenty of evenings and mornings traveling around Atlanta and beyond, we decided it was time to bring something closer to home, right here in Dahlonega at Yahoola Creek Park.

We wanted to bring more variety to the local car scene and create a place where everyone could enjoy their love for cars without having to travel so far.

One night, while attending a car meet, we met someone who was attending his car to his very first cruise-in. He told us he didn't feel his car was good enough for a car show because he hadn't done much work to it. We understood that feeling. After attending different car meets ourselves, we knew how important it was to find a place where you could feel comfortable, included and accepted, no matter what you drive.

That conversation stuck with us.

We wanted to create a place where everyone feels welcome, included and appreciated. It doesn't matter what you drive, how much work you've put into it, or whether you're attending your very first car meet. We know people have busy lives and when someone takes time out of their day to come to one of our car meets and hang out with us, we want them to know we are genuinely glad they are there. Whether you bring your friends, your significant other, the whole family (kids included!), or simply show up on your own, we want you to feel right at home.

💟More Than Just Cars

North Georgia Street Heaters is about more than the vehicles in the parking lot. It's about the people who bring them, the conversations that start around them, and the friendships that grow along the way.

We love seeing familiar faces return, meeting new people, and watching our little gathering grow into a place where everyone can relax and enjoy themselves. Whether you're talking engines, checking out someone's latest modifications, or just enjoying a Saturday evening with family and friends, there's something here for everyone.

Come as you are, bring what you drive, and make yourself at home!🚗💟
