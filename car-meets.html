Meetup Information

🧭Location: Yahoola Creek Park, Dahlonega, Georgia

📍 Where to Park: Look for us near the disc golf course and pedestrian bridge at Yahoola Creek Park. You can enter from Mechanicsville Road or Captain McDonald Road. Depending on turnout, we may spread out into the surrounding parking spaces.

🚗Look for the red Hyundai Veloster with black honeycomb accents, its hood up, and strobe lights flashing. You've found the Street Heaters!🔥

🗓️Schedule: 1st and 3rd Saturdays of each month

⏱️Time: 6:00 PM - 9:00 PM

☀️🌧️Weather: Rain or shine!

🎫Admission: Free! Everyone is welcome!

Meet Rules & Etiquette

We're all here to have a great time, hang out with fellow car enthusiasts, and share our love for everything automotive! To keep our meets fun and welcoming for everyone, please floow a few simple rules:

  • No unnecessary engine revving to show off.
  • Absolutely no burnouts
  • No drama.
  • Respect the park, its property, and everyone visiting.

Remember, we're sharing the park with families and other visitors who are there to enjoy themselves, too. Let's be respectful, keep the area clean, and make sure North Georgia Street Heaters leaves a positive impression!
